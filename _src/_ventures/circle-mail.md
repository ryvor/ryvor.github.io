## Overview

Circle Mail is an email-focused system aimed at improving visibility, tracking, and organisation of communication.

It focuses on bridging the gap between sending emails and understanding what happens after — including delivery, interaction, and response behaviour.

---

## Purpose

- Track email interactions and engagement  
- Improve visibility across communication workflows  
- Link conversations to specific users or actions  
- Reduce reliance on fragmented inbox-based tracking  

---

## What I’m Building

- Email routing and logging systems  
- Identifier-based email tracking (e.g. tagged addresses)  
- Interaction tracking (opens, clicks, behaviour where possible)  
- Integration with internal systems and databases
