## Overview

Flooring Zen is an e-commerce project centred around selling flooring products in a clean, accessible, and user-friendly way.

The goal is to remove the confusion often associated with flooring purchases by providing clear information, structured product ranges, and a smooth buying experience.

---

## Focus

- Simplify flooring product selection  
- Improve clarity in product descriptions  
- Provide a modern, easy-to-use online store  
- Create a scalable foundation for growth  

---

## What I’m Building

- Structured product catalogue (ranges, categories, variants)  
- Clear and informative product pages  
- Consistent product descriptions and purchase notes  
- Streamlined customer journey from browsing to checkout  

---

## Technology

- **Platform:** WordPress (WooCommerce)  
- **Frontend Enhancements:** Custom UI and styling  
- **Backend:** Custom plugins and structure improvements  
- **Hosting:** Managed web hosting environment
