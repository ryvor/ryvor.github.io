## Overview

Nova and Nook is a print-on-demand brand built around strong visual identity, character design, and cohesive product aesthetics.

The brand blends playful design with a premium feel, aiming to create products that are both expressive and well-crafted.

---

## Focus

- Build a recognisable, character-driven brand  
- Create visually consistent product lines  
- Deliver high-quality, well-presented products  
- Combine creativity with scalable e-commerce  

---

## 🧸 Brand Identity

- **Nova** — bold, expressive, energetic  
- **Nook** — softer, calm, and playful  

Together, they create a balanced identity that can flex across different styles, moods, and product types.

---

## What I’m Building

- Print-on-demand product catalogue  
- Character-based designs and artwork  
- Cohesive visual branding across all products  
- Product listings and storefront presence  

---

## Technology

- **Platforms:** Etsy, Shopify (planned/active)  
- **Design:** Digital illustration and branding tools  
- **Production:** Print-on-demand services  
- **Workflow:** Design → mockup → publish → iterate
