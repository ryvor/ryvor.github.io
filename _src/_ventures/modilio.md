## Overview

Modilio is a 3D printing venture focused on creating and selling custom-designed products.

It combines product design, rapid prototyping, and e-commerce — enabling ideas to move quickly from concept to physical product.

---

## Focus

- Design and produce unique 3D printed products  
- Maintain high-quality prints and finishes  
- Experiment with product categories and niches  
- Build a scalable e-commerce presence  

---

## What I Do

- 3D modelling and product design  
- Rapid prototyping and iteration  
- Print production and quality control  
- Product listing and marketplace management  
- Branding and presentation  

---

## Technology

- **Design:** CAD / 3D modelling tools  
- **Hardware:** Bambu Lab P1S  
- **E-commerce:** Online marketplace platforms  
- **Workflow:** Iterative design → print → refine
